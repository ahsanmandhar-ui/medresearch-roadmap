The free-viewport rect (computeFreeViewport) is what the map camera should keep focused content inside. focusCameraOnFreeViewport re-centers a world focus point into the free area via the inverse of worldToScreen (screen = world*zoom + camera ⇒ camera = screenCenter − world*zoom), zoom unchanged. fitBoundsToFreeViewport mirrors core's fitBounds but against the free viewport (zoom = min(scaleX,scaleY)*padding, then center).
